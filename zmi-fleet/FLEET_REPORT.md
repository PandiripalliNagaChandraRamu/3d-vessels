## 3) Key Fleet Capabilities (from /our-capabilities & /jackup-barges & /support-vessels)

- Self-Propelled DP Jack-Up Barges: versatile stable platform, cost-efficient vs non-propelled barges/rigs, multiple moves without tugs, DP-2, reduced footprint.
- Offshore Support Vessels: PSV, AHTS, DSV, MPSV, Tow Tugs, Utility - Transportation, Supply, Towing, Repair, Maintenance, Subsea, Diving, Geotechnical, Offshore Wind.
- Offshore Renewable Energy: T&I, Construction (POB + crane for HVDC/OSS), O&M (gearbox/blade change), Geotechnical (boreholes/CPT via monohull/JUB with derricks).
- Subsea IRM / Diving Support: pipeline/riser/flowline/umbilical inspection, construction.

## 4) Technical Notes

- Jack-up specs: Water depth 30-80m, Leg length 61-137m, Deck 550-2500 m², Cranes 45T - 400T (single or twin 190T/300T), POB 126-500 (upgradable 500), DP-2 Kongsberg, Azimuth thrusters, Helideck 22.2m (S92A/S61N, CAP437).
- OSV specs: Length 43-65m, DWT 746-1683 MT, Deck 201-500 m², Bollard Pull up to 74T (Zakher Provider), Speed 9-13 kts, DP-1/DP-2 where noted.
